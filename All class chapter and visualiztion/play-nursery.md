# Visualizations: Play and Nursery

Band `PLAY_NURSERY`. **Companion and voice are mandatory** in every entry. Tap-only input (no typing). One idea per screen, very large touch targets, short bilingual narration, a sound cue on every correct tap, gentle "try again" on wrong taps (no negative sounds).

Format: `slug` `renderer` | what it shows | controls | tiers | notes

---

## Class Play

### play.01 Recognizing numbers 1 to 100
- **number-parade** `number-grid` | Numbers 1–10 march in; each one jumps, flashes and is spoken | tap a number to hear it | T1 | companion, voice
- **finger-count-1-10** `finger-hands` | Both hands; each finger lights as the number is said | tap a finger or "next" | T1 | companion shows the hands
- **count-animals** `object-counting` | Farm/jungle animals; tap each to count aloud, the last number is highlighted as "how many" | tap animals | T1–T2 (1–5, then 1–10) | arrow points; companion counts along
- **number-trace** `shape-canvas` | Draw the digit by following a glowing path, with a stroke-order animation | finger trace | T1–T2 | sound on finish
- **number-song-grid** `number-grid` | 1–100 grid fills in as a song plays; counting by tens lights the last column | play/pause | T2 | voice
- **find-the-number** `number-grid` | "Find 7": wrong taps wobble, right tap sparkles | tap | T1–T3 (1–10, 1–50, 1–100) | companion cheers
- **missing-number** `number-line` | A path of stepping stones with one stone missing | drag number stone | T1–T3 | companion jumps stone to stone
- **number-vs-quantity** `object-counting` | Match a numeral card to the right group of objects | drag card | T1–T2 | misconception: counting the same object twice
