==========================================================================
 MATH CONCEPT MAP - ORIGINAL DESIGN FOR 5 SCHOOL GROUPS
==========================================================================
Purpose : A set of original math concept ideas, activities, links between
          topics, and assessment ideas, organised by the school's own
          grouping. Inspired only by the general idea of a "knowledge graph"
          (topics connected by prerequisites). All names, activities and
          structures below are newly written and can be freely changed.

Groups  : G1 = Play & Nursery
          G2 = Class 1 to 5
          G3 = Class 6 to 8
          G4 = Class 9 & 10
          G5 = Class 11 & 12

How each group is organised:
  A. Big Ideas (the "map regions")
  B. Concept Cards (what to learn + a fresh angle to teach it)
  C. Links (what connects to what / prerequisites)
  D. Activities & Games
  E. Quick Checks (quiz / flashcard style)
  F. Real-life Angle
  G. Mini Project

Note on level tags used in cards:
  [Seed]  = first meeting with the idea
  [Grow]  = practising and connecting
  [Bloom] = using it flexibly / explaining it to others

==========================================================================
 GROUP 1 : PLAY & NURSERY  (about ages 3-5)
==========================================================================

A. BIG IDEAS
  1. Noticing quantity     (more, less, same)
  2. Shapes in the world
  3. Patterns and order
  4. Position and movement
  5. Comparing sizes
  6. First counting

B. CONCEPT CARDS
  Card N1  - "Many or Few?"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Compare two groups without counting, just by looking.
    New angle: Children feed a toy "hungry monster" with beans; the monster
               says which bowl has more by "sniffing" (teacher guides).
  Card N2  - "Number Friends 1-10"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Each number has a character with that many spots/buttons.
    New angle: Number is linked to a body part count (2 eyes, 5 fingers,
               10 toes) so counting feels personal.
  Card N3  - "Shape Detectives"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Circle, square, triangle, rectangle recognised in objects.
    New angle: A "shape hunt" around the classroom with a paper magnifier;
               the child sticks a coloured dot on each shape found.
  Card N4  - "Big, Bigger, Biggest"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Order three objects by size.
    New angle: Line up three paper shoes (baby, child, giant) and match
               each to a character.
  Card N5  - "What Comes Next?"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Simple AB and AAB patterns.
    New angle: Patterns made with claps, stomps and jumps first, then with
               beads, then with colours on paper.
  Card N6  - "Up, Down, Inside, Beside"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Position words.
    New angle: A "treasure box" game: hide a toy and give clues like
               "under the chair".
  Card N7  - "Count and Take Away"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Adding one more / taking one away with objects up to 5.
    New angle: Story-play with a toy bus: 3 dolls get on, 1 gets off.
  Card N8  - "Heavy or Light?"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Feel and compare weight.
    New angle: A homemade balance from a hanger and two cups.

C. LINKS
  Many/Few  -> First counting -> Number friends -> Count and take away
  Shape detectives -> Patterns (shape patterns) -> Sorting by shape
  Big/Bigger -> Heavy/Light -> Measuring later in Class 1
  Position words -> Directions later in Class 1-2

D. ACTIVITIES & GAMES
  1. Counting Hopscotch      : jump on numbered squares, say the number.
  2. Sorting Basket Race     : sort buttons by colour or size into trays.
  3. Shape Bingo             : picture cards instead of numbers.
  4. Pattern Parade          : children stand in a pattern (sit, stand, sit).
  5. Water Play Measuring    : fill small and large cups, see which holds more.
  6. Clay Numbers            : roll clay into number shapes.
  7. Story Counting          : "Five little birds..." with finger puppets.
  8. Mystery Bag             : feel a shape inside a bag and guess it.

E. QUICK CHECKS (oral / picture based)
  - Point to the group that has more.
  - Which shape is round with no corners?
  - Clap-clap-stomp, clap-clap-?  What comes next?
  - Put 4 beads in the bowl.
  - Which toy is the smallest?
  Flashcard idea: picture on front (3 apples), numeral and word on back.

F. REAL-LIFE ANGLE
  Sharing snacks equally, counting steps to the gate, finding round things
  at home, tidying toys into groups.

G. MINI PROJECT
  "My Number Pocket" - each child collects 1 leaf, 2 stones, 3 seeds ...
  up to 5 in a small box and shows it to the class.

==========================================================================
 GROUP 2 : CLASS 1 TO 5
==========================================================================

A. BIG IDEAS
  1. Number sense and place value
  2. The four operations
  3. Fractions as parts of a whole
  4. Measurement (length, mass, capacity, time, money)
  5. Shapes, symmetry and space
  6. Data and simple chance
  7. Patterns and early algebra thinking

B. CONCEPT CARDS

  --- Class 1-2 ---
  Card P1  - "Ten-Frame Town"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Make numbers to 20 using ten-frames.
    New angle: Each frame is a "house" with 10 windows; numbers are
               families moving in and out.
  Card P2  - "Place Value Shop"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Ones, tens, hundreds.
    New angle: A pretend shop sells loose sticks (ones) and bundles (tens);
               customers must pay exact amounts.
  Card P3  - "Add Up Stories"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Addition and subtraction within 100.
    New angle: Children write their own picture stories, then the partner
               turns the story into a number sentence.
  Card P4  - "Skip-Count Trail"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Counting in 2s, 5s, 10s.
    New angle: Walk a floor trail; every 5th tile is painted, and the child
               predicts the next painted tile.
  Card P5  - "Time Tellers"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Hour, half hour, quarter hour.
    New angle: Create a cardboard clock for a daily routine chart.

  --- Class 3-4 ---
  Card P6  - "Array Gardens"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Multiplication as rows and columns.
    New angle: Plant paper flowers in rows; show 3x4 and 4x3 are the same
               garden turned around.
  Card P7  - "Equal Share Kitchen"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Division as sharing and grouping.
    New angle: Two ways to answer "12 biscuits, 3 plates": how many on each
               plate, and how many plates if each holds 3.
  Card P8  - "Fraction Folding"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Halves, quarters, thirds with paper strips.
    New angle: Fold a ribbon and compare lengths to see which fraction is
               bigger before using symbols.
  Card P9  - "Perimeter Walk"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Distance around a shape.
    New angle: Walk around the playground edge and count steps; compare to
               a rectangle drawn on paper.
  Card P10 - "Money Maker"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Coins, notes, change.
    New angle: A class market day with price tags and a "bank" for change.

  --- Class 5 ---
  Card P11 - "Fraction Number Line"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Place fractions and mixed numbers on a line.
    New angle: A clothesline in the classroom with fraction cards pegged in
               order.
  Card P12 - "Decimal Meter Stick"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Tenths and hundredths linked to metres, centimetres.
    New angle: Measure objects in metres with decimals, e.g. 0.45 m.
  Card P13 - "Area Tiles"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Area as covering with unit squares.
    New angle: Cover a book cover with sticky notes, then switch to
               multiplication for rectangles.
  Card P14 - "Data Detectives"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Tally, bar graph, pictogram.
    New angle: Survey "favourite lunch" and decide what the cook should
               prepare most.
  Card P15 - "Chance Cards"  [Seed]
    Idea   : Certain, likely, unlikely, impossible.
    New angle: Draw from a bag of coloured counters; sort statements onto a
               "chance ladder".
  Card P16 - "Mystery Number Machine"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Input-output rules (early algebra).
    New angle: A cardboard machine "doubles then adds 1"; others guess the
               rule from the outputs.

C. LINKS
  Ten-frames -> Place value -> Add/subtract -> Multiplication arrays ->
  Division sharing -> Fractions -> Decimals -> Area -> Algebra rules
  Skip-counting -> Multiplication tables -> Patterns -> Function machines
  Perimeter -> Area -> Volume (Group 3)
  Data detectives -> Averages (Group 3)
  Chance cards -> Probability (Group 3-4)

D. ACTIVITIES & GAMES
  1. Number Line Jump        : hop along a floor number line for add/subtract.
  2. Tables Tug              : two teams answer table facts to pull a rope marker.
  3. Fraction Pizza Build    : paper pizzas cut into slices to make orders.
  4. Shape Hunt Photo Walk   : photograph rectangles, circles, cubes outdoors.
  5. Clock Relay             : run to set the time the teacher calls out.
  6. Estimation Jar          : guess beans in a jar, then check by grouping.
  7. Mirror Art              : make symmetrical butterflies and name the axis.
  8. Mental Maths Bingo      : answers on the card, questions called aloud.

E. QUICK CHECKS
  - Show 47 using tens sticks and ones.
  - Which is greater: 3/4 or 2/4? Prove it with a drawing.
  - A shape has 4 equal sides and 4 right angles. Name it.
  - What is the missing number: 6 x __ = 42?
  - Read the bar graph and say which item is the least liked.
  Flashcard idea: front shows a clock face, back shows the time in words.

F. REAL-LIFE ANGLE
  Pocket money, reading a bus schedule, sharing cake, measuring a plant's
  growth every week, comparing prices in a shop.

G. MINI PROJECT
  "My Class Shop" : design price lists, make a bill, give change, and draw a
  bar graph of the most sold items.

==========================================================================
 GROUP 3 : CLASS 6 TO 8
==========================================================================

A. BIG IDEAS
  1. Integers and rational numbers
  2. Ratio, proportion and percentage
  3. Algebra foundations (expressions, equations)
  4. Geometry: lines, angles, triangles, congruence
  5. Mensuration (area, volume)
  6. Data handling and probability
  7. Exponents, factors and number theory

B. CONCEPT CARDS

  --- Class 6 ---
  Card M1  - "Factor Forest"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Factors, multiples, primes, HCF, LCM.
    New angle: Draw factor trees and treat primes as the "seeds" every
               number grows from.
  Card M2  - "Integer Elevator"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Negative and positive numbers.
    New angle: A building with basement floors; moving up and down models
               addition and subtraction of integers.
  Card M3  - "Ratio Recipes"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Ratio and unitary method.
    New angle: Scale a recipe for 4 people up to 30, and spot which
               ingredient breaks the ratio.
  Card M4  - "Variable Boxes"  [Seed]
    Idea   : A letter stands for an unknown number.
    New angle: A mystery box that hides a number; build expressions by
               combining boxes and loose counters.

  --- Class 7 ---
  Card M5  - "Equation Balance"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Solve linear equations by doing the same to both sides.
    New angle: Use a virtual see-saw; students must justify each move.
  Card M6  - "Angle Hunter"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Angle pairs, parallel lines and transversal.
    New angle: Use a road-map with crossing roads to find matching angles.
  Card M7  - "Triangle Rules"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Angle sum, inequality, congruence conditions.
    New angle: Try to build triangles from straws; discover which side
               lengths fail.
  Card M8  - "Percent Lens"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Percentages, discount, profit and loss, simple interest.
    New angle: Compare two shops' sales and decide the better deal.
  Card M9  - "Rational Number Map"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Fractions and decimals on one number line, operations.
    New angle: Locate numbers between two given rationals to show there are
               always more.

  --- Class 8 ---
  Card M10 - "Exponent Growth"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Laws of exponents, standard form.
    New angle: A rumour spreading: 2 people tell 2 more each hour; write the
               count as a power.
  Card M11 - "Pattern to Formula"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Algebraic identities and factorisation.
    New angle: Show (a+b)^2 with a square cut into four pieces.
  Card M12 - "Square and Cube Roots"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Perfect squares/cubes, estimation of roots.
    New angle: Tile squares on a grid and find the side from the area.
  Card M13 - "Quadrilateral Family Tree"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Properties of parallelogram, rhombus, rectangle, trapezium.
    New angle: Build a "family tree" showing which shape is a special case
               of another.
  Card M14 - "Surface and Volume"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Cuboid, cylinder; surface area and volume.
    New angle: Wrap a box with paper, measure, and compare with the formula.
  Card M15 - "Data Stories"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Mean, median, mode, pie chart, histogram.
    New angle: Choose the best summary for a real data set and defend why.
  Card M16 - "Direct vs Inverse"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Direct and inverse variation.
    New angle: Compare "more workers, less time" with "more distance, more
               time" using tables and graphs.

C. LINKS
  Factors/Primes -> HCF, LCM -> Fractions -> Rational numbers -> Algebra
  Integers -> Equations -> Coordinate graphs (Group 4)
  Ratio -> Percentage -> Interest -> Compound growth (Group 4-5)
  Ratio -> Direct/inverse variation -> Functions (Group 4)
  Triangles -> Congruence -> Similarity (Group 4) -> Trigonometry (Group 4)
  Area -> Surface area -> Volume -> Calculus applications (Group 5)
  Exponent growth -> Logarithms (Group 4-5)
  Mean/median/mode -> Statistics (Group 4-5)

D. ACTIVITIES & GAMES
  1. Prime Sieve Poster      : class colours a number grid to find primes.
  2. Integer War Cards       : red and black cards for negative/positive.
  3. Algebra Tile Puzzle     : build and simplify expressions with tiles.
  4. Geoboard Challenges     : build shapes of a given area or perimeter.
  5. Estimate the Percent    : guess percentages of shaded diagrams.
  6. Paper Folding Proofs    : fold to show angle sum of a triangle.
  7. Real Data Day           : measure height, hand-span, then analyse.
  8. Equation Escape Room    : each solved equation gives a lock digit.

E. QUICK CHECKS
  - Write the first five multiples of 6 and 8; find their LCM.
  - Solve: 3x - 7 = 11. Explain each step in words.
  - A shirt costs 800; after a 15% discount what is the price?
  - Can sides 3, 4 and 8 form a triangle? Why or why not?
  - Find the mean and median of 4, 7, 7, 9, 13.
  Flashcard idea: front shows an expression, back shows its factorised form
  and a picture area model.

F. REAL-LIFE ANGLE
  Phone data plans, sales discounts, scaling maps, cooking quantities,
  sports averages, bank interest, building materials.

G. MINI PROJECT
  "Design a Dream Room" : plan the floor with accurate scale, calculate
  area and paint cost, and present a budget with percentages.

==========================================================================
 GROUP 4 : CLASS 9 & 10
==========================================================================

A. BIG IDEAS
  1. Number systems and real numbers
  2. Polynomials and algebraic structure
  3. Linear and quadratic relations
  4. Coordinate geometry
  5. Euclidean geometry and proof
  6. Similarity and trigonometry
  7. Mensuration (advanced)
  8. Statistics and probability
  9. Sequences and progressions

B. CONCEPT CARDS

  --- Class 9 ---
  Card S1  - "Between the Numbers"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Irrational numbers and the real line.
    New angle: Construct sqrt(2) on a number line with a right triangle and
               discuss why it never ends.
  Card S2  - "Polynomial Anatomy"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Degree, terms, zeros, remainder and factor theorem.
    New angle: Treat a polynomial as a machine and find which inputs give
               zero output.
  Card S3  - "Line Equations Lab"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Linear equations in two variables and their graphs.
    New angle: Plot a taxi fare (fixed + per km) and interpret the slope as
               cost per kilometre.
  Card S4  - "Proof Builder"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Axioms, theorems, two-column proofs.
    New angle: Provide shuffled proof statements; students reorder them and
               justify each link.
  Card S5  - "Circle Secrets"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Chords, arcs, angle in a semicircle, cyclic quadrilateral.
    New angle: Use a dynamic sketch or string-and-pin model to test angle
               properties.
  Card S6  - "Heron's Pathway"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Area of triangle by Heron's formula.
    New angle: Compare with the base-height method to see when each is
               easier.
  Card S7  - "Statistics Lens"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Frequency tables, grouped data, central tendency.
    New angle: Analyse real exam or sports scores and find outliers.

  --- Class 10 ---
  Card S8  - "Two Equations, One Answer"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Pair of linear equations (graphical, substitution, elimination).
    New angle: Model two phone plans and find when they cost the same.
  Card S9  - "Quadratic Curve Story"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Solving quadratics (factorising, formula, completing the square)
               and graphs.
    New angle: A thrown ball path; interpret roots as landing points.
  Card S10 - "Sequences that Climb"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Arithmetic progressions, nth term, sum.
    New angle: Seating in a theatre where each row adds the same seats.
  Card S11 - "Similar Shapes, Same Story"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Similar triangles, Thales' theorem.
    New angle: Measure a tree height with a stick and shadow.
  Card S12 - "Trig Triangle Toolkit"  [Grow]
    Idea   : sin, cos, tan, identities, heights and distances.
    New angle: Use a homemade clinometer to measure a building angle.
  Card S13 - "Coordinate Compass"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Distance, midpoint, section formula.
    New angle: Plot a city map and compute shortest direct distances.
  Card S14 - "Surface Area Studio"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Combined solids, frustum, conversion between shapes.
    New angle: Design a toy rocket made of a cone, cylinder and hemisphere.
  Card S15 - "Probability Lab"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Theoretical vs experimental probability.
    New angle: Run 100 coin or dice trials and compare with predictions.
  Card S16 - "Tangent Touch"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Tangent to a circle and its properties.
    New angle: Roll a coin against a ruler and see where it touches once.

C. LINKS
  Real numbers -> Polynomials -> Quadratics -> Functions (Group 5)
  Linear equations -> Coordinate geometry -> Straight lines (Group 5)
  Proof builder -> All geometry -> Vectors and 3D (Group 5)
  Similar triangles -> Trigonometry -> Heights/distances -> Waves (Group 5)
  AP -> Series (Group 5) -> Limits and sums
  Statistics lens -> Dispersion, correlation (Group 5)
  Probability lab -> Conditional probability (Group 5)
  Circle secrets -> Conic sections (Group 5)

D. ACTIVITIES & GAMES
  1. Graph Matching Cards    : match equations to graphs and word stories.
  2. Trig Scavenger Hunt     : measure real heights around school.
  3. Quadratic Factor Duel   : teams race to factorise on cards.
  4. Proof Jigsaw            : puzzle pieces are statements and reasons.
  5. Geometry Art            : create a tessellation with measured angles.
  6. Probability Casino Day  : fair vs unfair games analysed with data.
  7. Spreadsheet Sprint      : use a sheet for AP sums, mean, and plots.
  8. Error Spotting Court    : find the mistake in a wrong solution.

E. QUICK CHECKS
  - Is 0.101001000... rational or irrational? Explain.
  - Factorise x^2 - 5x + 6 and state the roots.
  - Find the 20th term of 5, 9, 13, ...
  - In a right triangle with opposite 5 and hypotenuse 13, find sin and cos.
  - Prove that angles in the same segment of a circle are equal (outline).
  Flashcard idea: front shows a quadratic graph, back lists roots, vertex
  and direction of opening.

F. REAL-LIFE ANGLE
  Budget planning with two-variable equations, projectile paths in sports,
  survey analysis, architecture and ramps, loan payments, GPS distances.

G. MINI PROJECT
  "Measure Our School" : use trigonometry and similarity to estimate heights
  of buildings and flag pole, compare with a tape measure where possible,
  and report errors.

==========================================================================
 GROUP 5 : CLASS 11 & 12
==========================================================================

A. BIG IDEAS
  1. Sets, relations and functions
  2. Trigonometric functions and equations
  3. Algebra: complex numbers, inequalities, permutations, binomial theorem
  4. Sequences and series
  5. Coordinate geometry and conic sections
  6. Vectors and 3D geometry
  7. Limits, continuity, differentiation
  8. Integration and differential equations
  9. Matrices and determinants
  10. Statistics and probability
  11. Mathematical reasoning and modelling

B. CONCEPT CARDS

  --- Class 11 ---
  Card H1  - "Set Logic Garden"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Sets, Venn diagrams, relations.
    New angle: Use survey data on student clubs to explore overlap, union
               and complement.
  Card H2  - "Function Zoo"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Domain, range, one-one, onto, inverse, composition.
    New angle: Treat each function as an animal with habits (growth,
               symmetry, restrictions) and sort them.
  Card H3  - "Unit Circle Story"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Radian measure, trig functions, identities.
    New angle: A Ferris wheel that links angle to height over time.
  Card H4  - "Complex Plane Walk"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Complex numbers, modulus, argument, Argand diagram.
    New angle: Multiplication as rotation plus scaling on the plane.
  Card H5  - "Counting Strategies"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Permutations, combinations, binomial expansion.
    New angle: Plan seating, passwords and team selection; connect Pascal's
               triangle to combination counts.
  Card H6  - "Conic Garden"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Circle, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola.
    New angle: Slice a cone model (or torch beam on wall) to see each curve.
  Card H7  - "Limit Zoom"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Limits and the idea of a derivative.
    New angle: Zoom into a graph until it looks straight; the slope there is
               the derivative.
  Card H8  - "Series Machines"  [Grow]
    Idea   : GP, sum to infinity, special series.
    New angle: Bouncing ball whose heights form a geometric series.
  Card H9  - "Dispersion Dial"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Variance, standard deviation.
    New angle: Compare two classes with the same mean but different spread.

  --- Class 12 ---
  Card H10 - "Derivative Toolkit"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Rules of differentiation, chain, implicit, parametric.
    New angle: Build a "derivative recipe book" mapping each rule to a
               visual and a real rate.
  Card H11 - "Optimise It"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Maxima, minima, rate of change, curve sketching.
    New angle: Design the cheapest can or the largest-area fence.
  Card H12 - "Area Under the Story"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Definite integral as accumulated change; area between curves.
    New angle: Speed-time graph to distance; water-flow graph to volume.
  Card H13 - "Integration Strategies"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Substitution, parts, partial fractions.
    New angle: Decision flowchart that helps pick a method.
  Card H14 - "Differential Equations in Motion"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Forming and solving first-order ODEs.
    New angle: Model population growth, cooling coffee, or loan balance.
  Card H15 - "Matrix Machines"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Matrices, determinants, inverse, linear systems.
    New angle: Image transformations (rotate, reflect, scale) as matrices.
  Card H16 - "Vector Space Voyage"  [Grow]
    Idea   : Vectors, dot and cross products, lines and planes in 3D.
    New angle: Navigation of a drone: position vectors and direction
               vectors.
  Card H17 - "Probability Tree Lab"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, distributions.
    New angle: Medical test or spam filter scenario with tree diagrams.
  Card H18 - "Linear Programming Planner"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Feasible region and optimum.
    New angle: A small factory choosing product mix for maximum profit.
  Card H19 - "Proof and Reasoning"  [Bloom]
    Idea   : Induction, contradiction, logical statements.
    New angle: Prove a domino-chain pattern and discuss why base case
               matters.

C. LINKS
  Sets -> Relations -> Functions -> Limits -> Derivatives -> Optimisation
  Derivatives <-> Integrals (fundamental theorem) -> ODEs -> Modelling
  Unit circle -> Trig equations -> Waves -> Complex numbers (Euler idea)
  Counting -> Binomial -> Probability -> Distributions -> Statistics
  Coordinate geometry -> Conics -> Vectors -> 3D geometry
  Matrices -> Determinants -> Linear systems -> Linear programming
  Series -> Limits -> Convergence -> Calculus approximations
  Reasoning -> Induction -> Formal proof across all topics

D. ACTIVITIES & GAMES
  1. Graph Transformation Lab : shift, stretch, reflect functions and note
                                effects on the formula.
  2. Optimisation Challenge   : design a container with minimum material.
  3. Matrix Cipher            : encode and decode messages using matrices.
  4. Probability Court Case   : evaluate evidence using Bayes' theorem.
  5. Integral Detective       : choose the correct technique for a mystery
                                integral and defend it.
  6. Modelling Day            : fit a curve to real data (temperature,
                                population) and predict.
  7. Peer Teaching Rounds     : each student explains a theorem in 3 minutes.
  8. Error Autopsy            : analyse a flawed solution to find logical
                                gaps.

E. QUICK CHECKS
  - Find the domain and range of f(x) = sqrt(4 - x^2).
  - Differentiate x^2 sin x and explain the rule used.
  - Evaluate the integral of 2x from 0 to 3 and interpret it as an area.
  - A bag has 3 red and 2 blue balls; two are drawn without replacement.
    Find P(both red).
  - Solve the system using a matrix inverse: x + y = 5, 2x - y = 1.
  Flashcard idea: front shows a curve with a tangent line, back states the
  derivative idea and a real-life meaning.

F. REAL-LIFE ANGLE
  Engineering design, population forecasting, medical test accuracy,
  cryptography, economics (cost and revenue), computer graphics, physics
  motion, machine learning basics.

G. MINI PROJECT
  "Model a Real System" : choose an event (cooling tea, school fee growth,
  rainfall) -> collect data -> choose a function -> use derivative/integral
  ideas -> predict -> evaluate error -> present.

==========================================================================
 CROSS-GROUP IDEAS (USEFUL FOR WHOLE SCHOOL)
==========================================================================

1. SKILL SPIRAL
   Each big idea returns in every group at a deeper level:
   Counting (G1) -> Number sense (G2) -> Rational numbers (G3) ->
   Real numbers (G4) -> Complex numbers (G5).

2. THREE QUESTION TYPES TO USE EVERYWHERE
   - Recall    : "What is...?"
   - Reasoning : "Why does ... work?"
   - Reality   : "Where would we use ...?"

3. WEEKLY MATH ROUTINES
   - Number of the Day (G1-G3)
   - Which One Doesn't Belong? (G2-G4)
   - Estimation Station (all)
   - Error of the Week (G3-G5)
   - Problem Poster (all)

4. ASSESSMENT MIX
   - Quick oral/pictorial checks (G1)
   - Short quizzes and games (G2-G3)
